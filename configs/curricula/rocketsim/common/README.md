# Rocket League curricula

The `rocketsim` backend's curricula: Rocket League's soccar, one match per episode, 1v1, 2v2 or 3v3. The subject drives a blue car and bots drive the others: a team's cars take turns at the ball, the nearest going for it and the others waiting behind it, and the subject's car counts as one of its team's, so its teammates leave the ball to it while it is nearest. `levels/1v1.json`, `2v2.json` and `3v3.json` include `common/soccar.json` (a 5:00 match, the keyboard's controls, the instructions) and name the env's `team_size` and their title (README, "Shared phases"). Each also comes in a `*-GMPD3-stick-nav` version, the same match on a controller: it includes `common/soccar-GMPD3-stick-nav.json`, and plays on a rig whose `"inputs"` list the GMPD3 or an Xbox controller.

## Setup

`uv sync --extra rocketsim` installs [rocketsim-gym](https://github.com/hyruuk/rocketsim-gym) at the commit pyproject.toml pins, and RocketSim's wheel: macOS 14+ on arm64, Linux (x86_64, aarch64) or Windows x86_64. The frames are drawn offscreen with OpenGL 3.3; on Linux that is Mesa's EGL (`libegl1 libegl-mesa0 libgl1-mesa-dri`), which needs no display. The arena is rocketsim-gym's homemade one, built from Rocket League's published field dimensions: its curves, corners and goals are close to the game's, not the same.

## Buttons

The env's action is MultiBinary(12), a controller's buttons: 0 throttle, 1 reverse, 2 stick left, 3 stick right (steer on the ground, yaw in the air), 4 stick up (nose down), 5 stick down (nose up), 6 jump, 7 boost, 8 powerslide (in the air, the stick left / right rolls), 9 ball cam (a press switches between car cam and ball cam), 10 air roll left, 11 air roll right. Held buttons combine, and each stick button is the stick pushed all the way. `soccar.json` keeps Rocket League's PC keys W / S (throttle, reverse), A / D (steer) and Q / E (air roll). A key holds one button here, so pitch, which Rocket League also puts on W / S, has keys of its own (UP / DOWN); jump and boost, on the mouse in Rocket League, are on SPACE and LEFT SHIFT, which moves powerslide to LEFT CTRL and ball cam to C. `soccar-GMPD3-stick-nav.json` keeps Rocket League's controller defaults on the controls a GMPD3 has: RT throttle, LT reverse, the stick to steer and pitch, A jump, B boost, X powerslide, Y ball cam; air roll left and right, which Rocket League leaves unbound on a controller, have no control. Each trigger and each side of the stick is a button, held from half its travel (the stick's through its controller's calibration, README "Calibrate each controller once per rig"), so the car steers and pitches all the way or not at all, as on the keyboard. Keep each shared phase's Controls text and controls image (`configs/images/controls/rocketsim/`) in sync by hand.

## Timing

Physics runs at 120 Hz and a step advances `ticks_per_step` (2) ticks, so the game plays at its real speed at 60 fps; a phase at another fps plays faster or slower, and the run says so. On an Apple M4 a step takes 0.1 ms in 1v1 and 0.25 ms in 3v3, and a 1024x576 frame about 3 ms.

## Logging

Per frame: the action, the reward (+1 for each blue goal, -1 for each orange one), and the env's `sample_fields`: the match's phase, clock, overtime and score, the goal scored during the step, whether ball cam is on, the ball, and for each car, under its name (`blue1`, the subject's, to `orange3`), its position, velocity, rotation, boost, ground and supersonic flags, demolition, the touches and demolitions of the step, and the controls it obeyed. Every frame is stored, about 48 KB each at 1024x576 (170 MB a minute). The block's `block_end` record carries the end state and the env's provenance: package versions, the collision meshes' SHA-256s, the cars a team, the tick rates, the OpenGL renderer and the camera and controller settings. A block replays bit for bit from `episode_seeds` and the actions on a machine where those are the same.
